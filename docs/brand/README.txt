TERRIS - LOGO PACK (Summit Flag)

Meaning
  A coral flag planted on the curve of the earth (a teal hemisphere with a
  latitude/meridian grid): calm pride in the places you have stood.

Colours
  Coral (visited / flag)   #E8613C   dark mode #FF7A55
  Teal (lived / earth)     #1F7A8C   dark mode #3FB1C4
  Teal shade (grid lines)  #155A68   dark mode #2A8394
  Amber (want to go)       #F2B134
  Ink (pole, wordmark)     #17212B   on dark: Canvas #F7F5F0
  Canvas                   #F7F5F0   dark canvas #0F1418

Wordmark
  "Terris" in Outfit Bold (SIL OFL), tracking -1% (-0.01em), font kerning on.
  Outlined to paths in every SVG; no font needed.
  Horizontal lockup: cap height = 58% of mark height, baseline on the dome's base.
  Stacked lockup: cap height = 42% of mark height, centred under the mark.

Clear space
  x = the flag's height (about 1/4 of the mark's height) on every side.

Minimum sizes
  Mark 16 px (favicon) . horizontal lockup 24 px tall . stacked lockup 56 px tall.
